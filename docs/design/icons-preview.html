Christmas lights: the C9 bulb

Classic opaque C9s hanging point-down from a green socket and clip: a fat teardrop, widest and round near the socket, with a soft rounded point, a lighter centre and a highlight. Static colours, nothing flashes. i-c9 takes its glass from --bulb (default currentColor, edge --bulb-edge) and its socket from --icon-accent; i-c9-red|yellow|blue|green|orange are fixed classic colours. The garland is garland-lights.svg (300 px tile, repeat-x) and garland-lights-corner.svg, each with a -dark file.
