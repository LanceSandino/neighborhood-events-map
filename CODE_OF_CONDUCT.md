# Code of conduct

This project helps neighbors put their homes on a map for community events. We want working on it to feel the same way those events should: welcoming, safe and kind.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to open source, to self-hosting or to the language you are writing in.
- Assume good intent, discuss the work and not the person, and accept a decision once it is made (see `DECISIONS.md` for how to supersede one with new evidence).
- Respect privacy. Never post residents' names, emails, addresses or a neighborhood's private data in issues, pull requests, screenshots or logs; use the fictional demo community instead.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualised language or imagery, discrimination of any kind, sharing someone's private information without their permission, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any project space where you represent the project.

Report a problem privately to the maintainers: use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on this repository (Security → Report a vulnerability) and say that it concerns conduct, or ask in an issue for a private contact without giving details. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that break this code and may block people who do so repeatedly or seriously.
